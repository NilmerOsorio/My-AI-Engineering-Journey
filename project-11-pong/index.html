Neon Pong arena

A neon Pong court. Player 1 controls the left paddle with W and S, or with the up and down arrow keys in PvC mode. In local PvP, Player 2 controls the right paddle with the up and down arrow keys. In PvC, the computer controls the right paddle. On touch devices, drag on your half of the court or use the on-screen up and down buttons. First to 5 points wins. Press Space or Escape to pause.
